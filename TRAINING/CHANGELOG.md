# Changelog

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-25

Fictitious competitors at a level: a final qualification score plus or minus a delta, an average
per arrow (duels by sets) or a match score (duels by points) plus or minus a delta, refused beyond
what the face and the arrows allow. Live page: while it is open, the simulated competitors shoot
at the pace of the archers of the training, in the qualification and in the duels, through the
core's own functions; the dashboard reminds the coach to keep it open, and each screen chooses
the events it shows. Training from a World Archery competition, the second way of "Create a
training": search by period, country, discipline, level and name, archers ticked on the same page
in the real competition's categories; the real events,
athletes, teams and results; duels played "reality first" (real meeting, own arrows of the phase,
arrows of the one who eliminated them). In every training the winners go on to the next round without a click, and a shoot-offs page
in the coach's menu takes the shoot-off arrows and the archer closest to the centre. The event
rankings now follow every change of score (they followed only arrivals and departures of archers).
"Launch every bracket" on the ranking page. Two new
tables and new columns, created on the first opening of a page of the module.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-24

Fictitious competitors with qualification scores drawn around an average per arrow (rankings, teams
and brackets, without a target nor a scorecard). Follow-up of the archers: trainings and real
competitions by situation, averages, best and last scores, duels won and lost, chart of the average
per arrow, CSV exports; the results of the trainings are kept by the module once a training is
deleted from ianseo. Two new tables, created on the first opening of a page of the module.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-23

First version: reference formats, regular archers and groups, creation of a training from a format
(date, starting time, programme moved, code YYMMDDXX), category per archer, teams and mixed teams
composed at any time, automatic placement on the targets, ranking and duels page (brackets filled
in the chosen order), simulated qualification round for duels only, coach's dashboard and
simplified menu per browser. Recurve by default, class from the date of birth with the French
season rule; import and export of formats, archers and groups. ISK-Lite set up on every training
(server address on the formats page), scorecards with the QR codes, placement in the order of the
AUTH module with its physical rules only, manual moves on the visual target plan, archers per target switched with one button, number of
targets adjusted to the archers with another, missing targets reported.
